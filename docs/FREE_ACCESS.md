# Free access and project funding

This is a promise about the official service at `scam-reports.org`. It is separate from the software license.

## The site stays free

The official service will not charge for or sell:

- subscriptions, premium accounts, paywalled reports, or evidence access;
- ranks, badges, staff roles, reputation, or moderation results;
- faster publication, appeal, correction, or removal;
- advertising profiles, personal data, or referrals tied to reported products;
- registration, reports, reviews, replies, appeals, or access to public records.

If money runs short, first cut optional costs, pause intake, switch to read-only, ask openly for support, or publish a shutdown plan before closing. Do not put core safety information behind a paywall.

## What "non-profit basis" means here

Money received for the official project pays project costs: hosting, storage, email, security, legal or compliance work, backups, accessibility, moderation tools, and community outreach. It is not distributed as dividends, owner withdrawals, or profit shares.

The project is not claiming that a registered charity, tax-exempt association, or other legal non-profit already exists. If one is formed, publish its legal name, jurisdiction, governing documents, and reporting setup before describing the site that way.

Maintainers may be reimbursed for documented expenses or receive publicly approved pay for necessary work. Payment buys no influence over reports, appeals, ranks, moderation, project priorities, or code review.

## Donations and sponsorship

Support is acceptable only when:

- it is voluntary and unlocks no access or status;
- the funder sees no private data or queue;
- the funder cannot direct a finding, removal, rank, appeal, or enforcement decision;
- conflicts are disclosed and handled by recusal;
- aggregate income and spending are reported at least once a year without exposing donors;
- money from a subject under review, or a party in an unresolved moderation conflict, is refused or returned.

The site does not need donations to launch.

## The AGPL is separate

`AGPL-3.0-or-later` allows commercial use and charging for copies or services. In general, an operator running a modified version over a network must offer its Corresponding Source. The license neither requires free hosting nor makes a project a charity.

This funding policy does not automatically apply to independent deployments. It is a governance promise for the official site. [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) is a practical summary; `LICENSE` controls.

## Changing this policy

The promises against subscriptions, paywalls, paid influence, and profit distribution cannot disappear in a quiet edit. A clarification needs a public issue, rationale, review, and versioned change. A proposal to weaken a promise needs community discussion in advance and cannot retroactively change duties owed to existing users or private data.

Applicable law, the AGPL, and the operator's privacy and moderation duties still apply.
