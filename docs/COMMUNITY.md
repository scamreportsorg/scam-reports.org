# Community and roles

You can help by documenting an incident, adding first-hand context, correcting a record, fixing the software, reviewing policy, or volunteering for moderation. None of that costs money or buys influence.

## Put things in the right place

- Reports, evidence, reviews, replies, corrections, and appeals go through the website.
- Code, bugs, features, docs, design, translations, accessibility, and governance go through GitHub.
- Security bugs go through [Private Vulnerability Reporting](../SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).

Never paste accusations, evidence, Discord IDs, email addresses, staff messages, or other personal data into a public issue or pull request.

For a case, give a clear timeline and say where each detail came from. Stick to first-hand facts or research somebody else can verify. Flag errors and duplicates instead of piling on. Sharing a report is fine; brigading somebody or presenting an allegation as a verdict is not.

For the code, use synthetic data and a focused reproduction. Tests, accessibility, translations, privacy controls, the forum UI, and practical policy feedback are all useful. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) has the workflow.

All reports, reviews, and replies pass moderation. Popularity does not make a claim true.

## Four separate systems

| System                | Meaning                                                     | Not the same as                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account role**      | `member`, `moderator`, or `admin`; controls site permission | Popularity or seniority                            |
| **Community rank**    | Milestones from approved contributions                      | Trustworthiness or staff power                     |
| **Report reputation** | A moderated score attached to a reported profile            | A member score, legal finding, or safety guarantee |
| **Repository role**   | Contributor or maintainer access to the source project      | Access to private reports or production            |

The [rank ladder](https://scam-reports.org/community/ranks) counts approved activity only. Pending, rejected, deleted, and duplicate activity earns nothing. Family deduplication and reply caps limit point farming. Points cannot be bought, transferred, redeemed, or used to influence moderation.

## Applying to moderate

Applications are private and start on the [account page](https://scam-reports.org/account). You need an active member account with Discord and email linked. [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) explains selection and role grants. [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#moderator-applications) defines how long answers and notes are kept.

## Administrator access

There is no public administrator application. Promotions follow [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md). Sensitive actions keep the checks in [AUTHENTICATION.md](AUTHENTICATION.md). Site staff do not receive repository ownership or release rights.

## Contributing code

Use synthetic data, add tests, and sign non-merge commits with `git commit --signoff`. Contributions normally use `AGPL-3.0-or-later`. A DCO sign-off confirms your right to submit the work; it does not transfer your copyright.

Repository permissions follow [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md). Even an excellent pull request creates no claim to staff, community rank, or production access.

Do not use this project for harassment, retaliation, doxxing, mass reporting, or automated takedowns. It is a reviewed archive with a correction route, not a punishment tool.
