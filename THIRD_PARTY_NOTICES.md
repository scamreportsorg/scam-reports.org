# Third-party notices

The Scam-Reports.org source is `AGPL-3.0-or-later`. Dependencies keep their own licenses.

`package-lock.json` is the dependency inventory. Releases include an SPDX SBOM built from that graph. CI rejects a package with no declared license or a license outside the reviewed allowlist.

The locked graph currently declares `0BSD`, `Apache-2.0`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `BlueOak-1.0.0`, `CC-BY-4.0`, `CC0-1.0`, `ISC`, `LGPL-3.0-or-later`, `MIT`, `MPL-2.0`, and `Python-2.0`, including choice and combined expressions. Creative Commons data and weak-copyleft libraries keep their upstream attribution and source-availability requirements.
